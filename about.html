Who we are

Founded by an electrical engineer and a mechanical engineer.

Brian Coldwell, PE and Oscar Sebelen, PE. Founding principals.

We founded RCI Engineering in 2005: Oscar Sebelen, PE, an electrical engineer and Brian Coldwell, PE, a mechanical engineer. Both hold NCEES records. Both are LEED Accredited Professionals. Brian is a Certified Commissioning Authority. Oscar is NICET certified.

We design the systems inside buildings: heating, ventilation and air conditioning, power and lighting, domestic water, sanitary and storm drainage, fuel gas, fire sprinklers and fire alarm plus the controls that tie them together. We provide commissioning, which verifies that those systems are installed and perform as designed.

We organize our work in three studios by building type. Our High-end Residential studio designs systems for homes and estates from Key Biscayne to Bonita Springs. Our Commercial studio covers luxury retail, restaurants, hotels, offices and medical buildings. Our Multifamily studio runs from six apartments in Bonita Springs to University Pointe in Davie, eight buildings and more than 700,000 sq ft under air.

Most of our work is in Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach counties. Our record also reaches Naples, Marco Island, Sarasota and Tallahassee plus two university buildings in True Blue, Grenada.

How we work

How a project runs.

Every one of our projects opens with an onboarding questionnaire and runs on structured weekly updates. The steps are the same on a 3,275 sq ft marina kitchen and on a 700,000 sq ft campus.

  1. OnboardingA questionnaire that captures the program, the budget and the deadlines.
  2. Basis of designSystems chosen with the architect to fit the design, the budget and the schedule.
  3. CoordinationStructured weekly updates while the drawings develop.
  4. Permit setCoordinated MEP and fire protection documents, signed and sealed.
  5. ConstructionAnswers to requests for information and submittal reviews, often within 48 hours.

Two documents carry the method.

  • The onboarding questionnaire

    Opens every project. It records the program, the budget and the deadlines, so the basis of design can be chosen against all three.

  • The weekly update

    Structured and written, while the drawings develop. It tells the architect what moved, what is coordinated and what is waiting on a decision.

Definitions

What we are and what we are not.

Three terms the market blurs. The site defines them so nobody, human or machine, assumes we are something it is not.

  • MEP design

    We design the mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection systems of a building and issue the drawings a contractor works from. We do not install, construct or sell equipment.

  • Commissioning

    A process that verifies a building's systems are installed and perform as the design intended, through design review, verification of the installation and functional testing. We provide it. Brian Coldwell, PE, is a Certified Commissioning Authority.

  • Milestone inspection

    A structural inspection of condominium and cooperative buildings under Florida Statutes section 553.899, performed by a licensed architect or engineer. It is a structural service and it is not MEP design.

Questions

Questions architects ask us.

Short answers that stand on their own. The full set is on the Questions page.

What does RCI Engineering do?

We are RCI Engineering. We design the mechanical, electrical, plumbing and fire protection systems inside buildings and provide commissioning. Architects design the buildings and contractors construct them. We engineer the systems that make them work: air, power, water, fire protection and the controls that tie them together. We were founded in 2005 and work from our office in Davie, Florida.

How are the systems kept within budget?

The budget enters at the start. Our onboarding questionnaire captures the program, the budget and the deadlines. At the basis of design, we choose the systems with the architect and the owner to fit all three before anything is drawn.

Which software does RCI use?

AutoCAD and Revit. Projects that call for building information modeling are modeled in Revit, as Six13, The Aldridge, The Laramore and Carriage House were. Others are drawn in AutoCAD.

Where does RCI work?

Our office is at 5599 S University Drive, Suite 204, in Davie, Florida. Most of our work is in South Florida, from Key Biscayne and Miami Beach to Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and Palm Beach. Our projects also reach Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, Stuart and Tallahassee. Outside Florida, we designed the systems for two university buildings in True Blue, Grenada. Our professional engineers are licensed in multiple states.

Do architects come back to RCI?

They do. Of the 39 projects in our 2026 studio portfolios, 28 came from clients who hired us more than once: eight architecture practices and a custom-home company. One practice brought us six projects from Stuart to Palm Beach: a hotel renovation, three restaurants, a marina kitchen and a retail suite. Another brought luxury retail stores from the Miami Design District to Bal Harbour, Palm Beach and Naples. Homes in Naples, Marco Island, Coral Gables, Miami Beach and Key Biscayne also came from architects who returned.
