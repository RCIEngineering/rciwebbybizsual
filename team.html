Founders

Founded in 2005 by an electrical engineer and a mechanical engineer.

Oscar Sebelen, PE

Founding Principal · Electrical Engineer

An electrical engineer trained at the Pontificia Universidad Católica Madre y Maestra in Santiago, Dominican Republic. He moved to Florida in 2002 and has worked since then in construction, management and design. He co-founded RCI in 2005.

Registered Professional Engineer · NCEES Council Record · LEED AP · NICET certified

Brian Coldwell, PE

Founding Principal · Mechanical Engineer

A mechanical engineer and a graduate of Florida State University, in Florida's construction industry since 2003. He has worked with firms and projects of many scales and co-founded RCI in 2005.

Registered Professional Engineer · LEED AP · NCEES Council Record (Model Law) · Certified Commissioning Authority (CxA) · Level II Thermographer, ITC
