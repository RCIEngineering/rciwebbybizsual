The entry

What every entry holds.

Six links in one chain. The three in the middle are what no local rival publishes.

  • Project fact

    What it is and where: building type, location, size, stories or units, studio, status and year.

  • Engineering scope

    The systems we designed, by discipline. What was mechanical, what was electrical, what was plumbing, what was fire protection.

  • Constraint

    What made it hard. One constraint, stated plainly.

  • Design response

    The engineering decision that answered it.

  • Coordination

    Who had to agree and when. One or two lines.

  • Outcome

    The part of the architecture that stayed as drawn. Only outcomes that can be shown.

What we ask for

One short list per project.

So that the engineers are approached once and know exactly what to give.

  • The facts

    Address or city, size, stories or units, status and year, from our project records.

  • The systems

    What was designed in each discipline, in a few lines from the engineer who led it.

  • The hard part

    The constraint and the decision that answered it, in the engineer's words.

  • The pictures

    A photograph or rendering with its rights cleared plus our own sheet for the space with the title block, seal and addresses removed.

  • The people

    Who led it at RCI.
