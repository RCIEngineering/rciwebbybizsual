One set

Drawn together. Issued together.

A building's systems compete for the same space above the ceiling and inside the walls. When they are drawn by separate offices, the architect ends up as the referee. At RCI the five disciplines sit in one office, so a duct crossing is checked against the beam, the pipe above it and the conduit beside it before the sheet leaves.

The sheets say who coordinates what. On a recent apartment renovation in Miami Beach, the air distribution table tells the contractor to coordinate slot diffuser borders and colors with the architect while the key notes require duct crossings to be checked against the structural drawings. On a retail store at Brickell City Centre, the key notes cite the 2023 Florida mechanical code section by section and set out the contractor's scope of work.

Projects that call for building information modeling are modeled in Revit, as Six13, The Aldridge, The Laramore and Carriage House were. Others are drawn in AutoCAD.

The set

What is in an RCI drawing set.

The product of an MEP firm is a set of drawings. This is what ours contains.

  • Floor plans

    Each discipline on the architect's background, with equipment, routes and devices located.

  • Legends and schedules

    The HVAC legend, the air distribution table, panel schedules, fixture schedules and equipment schedules.

  • Riser and one-line diagrams

    How power, water, sanitary, gas and sprinkler mains run through the building.

  • Details

    Supports, penetrations, curbs, connections and the HVHZ attachments where they apply.

  • Key notes

    What to coordinate, with whom and under which code section.

  • Calculations

    Loads, energy compliance forms, water and gas sizing, as the permit requires.

  • Seal

    Every set filed for permit is signed and sealed by a Florida professional engineer.

Codes

The codes we design to.

Named, because an answer engine can recognize a code and a visitor can check one.

  • Florida Building CodeThe edition in force when the permit is filed. Its Building, Mechanical, Plumbing, Fuel Gas and Energy Conservation volumes govern most of what we draw.
  • National Electrical CodeNFPA 70, as Florida adopts it through the Building Code. Services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and emergency systems.
  • Florida Fire Prevention CodeWith the NFPA standards it adopts: NFPA 13 for sprinklers, NFPA 72 for fire alarm, NFPA 101 for life safety.
  • High-Velocity Hurricane ZoneMiami-Dade and Broward counties. Rooftop equipment, curbs and attachments designed for the wind loads the zone requires.
  • Energy conservationThe Florida Building Code, Energy Conservation. Envelope, lighting power and equipment efficiency, documented with the compliance forms the permit needs.
  • Local authorityEach city and county reviews the set under its own amendments and utility rules. Our key notes cite the section the contractor has to meet.

Questions

Questions architects ask about the work.

The full set is on the Questions page.

What is in an RCI drawing set?

An RCI permit set covers each discipline in our scope, with floor plans, legends, schedules, details and key notes. Our mechanical floor plans carry an HVAC legend, an air distribution table and key notes that say what to coordinate and with whom. Retail sheets add the contractor's scope of work. Every set filed for permit is signed and sealed by a Florida professional engineer.

Which codes does RCI design to?

We design to the Florida Building Code in force when the permit is filed, with its mechanical, plumbing, fuel gas and energy conservation volumes. Electrical design follows the National Electrical Code as Florida adopts it. Fire protection follows the Florida Fire Prevention Code and the NFPA standards it adopts. In Miami-Dade and Broward counties, the High-Velocity Hurricane Zone adds requirements for equipment exposed to wind. Our key notes cite the code section the contractor has to meet.

How does RCI coordinate with the architect and the other consultants?

From the start. We set the basis of design with the architect. As the set develops, we coordinate routes, equipment and penetrations with the architectural and structural drawings. Our sheets say who coordinates what. On a recent apartment renovation in Miami Beach, the air distribution table tells the contractor to coordinate slot diffuser borders and colors with the architect. The key notes require duct crossings to be checked against the structural drawings. Projects that call for building information modeling are modeled in Revit, as Six13, The Aldridge and The Laramore in Fort Lauderdale were.

When should an architect bring RCI into a project?

As early as schematic design. Shafts, ceiling heights, equipment locations, roof screens and the electrical service all shape the architecture. Each is easier to place before the plans are fixed. A system decided early can be designed around the architecture. One decided late has to be fitted into it.

Does RCI stay involved during construction?

Yes. During construction administration we answer the contractor's requests for information and review submittals. Replies often come within 48 hours. For two university buildings in True Blue, Grenada, our scope ran from design through construction administration to completion.
