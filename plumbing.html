What it is

Plumbing design is water in, waste out and gas to the flame.

Plumbing engineering design covers domestic cold and hot water, water heating, sanitary drainage and venting, storm drainage, fuel gas from the meter or tank to each appliance, grease waste in kitchens, fixtures and the risers that carry all of it through the building.

Architects and developers rely on it on homes with several kitchens and a dozen bathrooms, on restaurants where the grease interceptor and the hood share a wall, on apartment buildings where every stack has to land in a chase and on offices and medical buildings with their own fixture counts. Contractors work from the plans, the risers and the schedules.

  • The program

    Kitchens, bars, spas, laundries, gyms and anything that uses water or gas.

  • The site and civil plans

    Where water, sewer and gas reach the building and where storm water goes.

  • The owner's requirements

    Fixtures, water heating preferences, gas appliances and what the budget allows.

What it prevents

Stacks that land in the chases the architect drew.

A plumbing set coordinated early keeps a stack from landing in the middle of a living room below, keeps the grease interceptor where the health inspector and the kitchen can both live with it, keeps the water heater out of the one closet the owner wanted and keeps the gas route from crossing a ceiling that has no room for it.

Questions

Questions architects ask about plumbing.

The full set is on the Questions page.

Does RCI design restaurant kitchens?

Yes. Grease waste and interceptors, gas to the cooking line, water to the fixtures and the drainage a commercial kitchen needs, coordinated with the hood and make-up air on the mechanical sheets. On the record: a marina kitchen in Stuart, a food hall in Miami and restaurants from Miami Beach to West Palm Beach.

Does RCI design gas systems?

Yes. Propane and natural gas from storage or meter to each appliance, in residences, restaurants and apartment buildings, under the Florida Building Code, Fuel Gas.

How are the plumbing risers coordinated with the architecture?

The stacks are placed in the chases the architect drew and checked floor by floor so that a bathroom above does not drain through a kitchen below. Sections show whether the slab and the ceiling cavity have room for the slopes the code requires.

Who signs the plumbing set?

A Florida professional engineer at RCI. Every set filed for permit is signed and sealed.
