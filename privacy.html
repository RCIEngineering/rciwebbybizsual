  • What is collected

    When you send a message through the contact or careers form, the site collects what you type: your name, email address, phone number and the message. Nothing else is asked.

  • Why

    To answer you. A message about a project goes to the office in Davie. An application goes to the people who hire.

  • Who sees it

    RCI Engineering, Inc. Messages are delivered by email through the service that sends them. They are not sold or shared for marketing.

  • Analytics

    The site measures visits in aggregate: pages viewed, the city a visit comes from and the device used. No advertising profiles are made.

  • Cookies

    Only what the analytics service and the browser need to work. The site sets no advertising cookies.

  • Your choices

    Write to info@rcieng.com to ask what the site holds about you or to have it deleted.
