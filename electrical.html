What it is

Electrical design is everything that plugs in, switches on or keeps running.

Electrical engineering design covers the service entrance and metering, switchboards and panels, feeders and branch circuits, lighting and lighting controls, receptacles and equipment connections, generators, transfer switches and uninterruptible power, battery storage plus the raceways that carry low-voltage systems through the building.

Architects and developers rely on it on every building type we design for. Lighting designers and interior designers coordinate fixtures and controls through it. Contractors work from the power and lighting plans, the one-line diagram and the panel schedules.

What it prevents

A service sized once, in the right place.

An electrical set coordinated early keeps a utility service from being undersized for the equipment the other disciplines add later, keeps the meter room and the generator where the architect can live with them and keeps fixtures and controls aligned with the reflected ceiling plan instead of fighting it.

On a campus or a garage, it also keeps the feeders short and the panels where the contractor can reach them.

Questions

Questions architects ask about electrical.

The full set is on the Questions page.

Does RCI design generators and battery storage?

Yes. Generators, transfer switches and uninterruptible power supply are part of the electrical scope where the program calls for them. A residence in Tallahassee carries battery storage and power walls.

Does RCI design lighting?

Yes, fixtures and controls as part of the electrical set, coordinated with the architect's ceilings and the energy code's lighting power limits. The largest lighting layout on the record is the sports-field lighting for a university campus in True Blue, Grenada.

How is the electrical service sized?

From the load: every piece of equipment the other disciplines add, the lighting, the receptacles and the program's special loads, with the demand factors the National Electrical Code allows. The size is confirmed with the utility early, because the service and the meter room shape the site plan.

Does RCI coordinate with the mechanical and plumbing scope?

In the same office. Every motor, heater, pump and condensing unit on the mechanical and plumbing sheets gets its circuit on the electrical sheets, drawn by the same team.
