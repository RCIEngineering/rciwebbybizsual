Questions

Questions people ask before they call.

The full set is on the Questions page.

How does a project with RCI start?

With a call to our office at 954-680-2690 or an email to info@rcieng.com, ideally with the drawings, the program and the schedule. Once we are engaged, the project opens with an onboarding questionnaire and runs on a written update every week.

Where does RCI work?

Our office is at 5599 S University Drive, Suite 204, in Davie, Florida. Most of our work is in South Florida, from Key Biscayne and Miami Beach to Fort Lauderdale, Boca Raton and Palm Beach. Our projects also reach Naples, Marco Island, Bonita Springs, Stuart and Tallahassee. Outside Florida, we designed the systems for two university buildings in True Blue, Grenada. Our professional engineers are licensed in multiple states.

When should an architect bring RCI into a project?

As early as schematic design. Shafts, ceiling heights, equipment locations, roof screens and the electrical service all shape the architecture. Each is easier to place before the plans are fixed. A system decided early can be designed around the architecture. One decided late has to be fitted into it.

What should I send with a first inquiry?

The floor plans and the reflected ceiling plans if they exist, the elevations and sections, the program, the location and the schedule. A sketch is enough to start the conversation. The onboarding questionnaire collects the rest once we are engaged.
