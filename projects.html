Returning clients

The architects who know us best keep coming back.

Of the 39 projects in our 2026 studio portfolios, 28 came from clients who hired us more than once: eight architecture practices and a custom-home company. One practice brought six projects from Stuart to Palm Beach: a hotel renovation, three restaurants, a marina kitchen and a retail suite. Another brought luxury retail stores from the Miami Design District to Bal Harbour, Palm Beach and Naples. Homes in Naples, Marco Island, Coral Gables, Miami Beach and Key Biscayne also came from architects who returned.
