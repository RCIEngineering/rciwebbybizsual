What it is

The bridge between design and operation.

Commissioning is the process that confirms a building's mechanical, electrical and plumbing systems are installed and perform as the design intended. A drawing says what a system should do. Commissioning measures what it does, system by system, before the owner takes the keys.

Owners and developers rely on it to take over a building that works. Architects rely on it so the systems serve the design. Lenders and certification programs often require it. We provide it through Brian Coldwell, PE, a Certified Commissioning Authority and a Level II Thermographer.

The phases

Five phases, each with something in your hands.

In our words, from the practice as it is published.

  1. Design reviewThe MEP design read against the owner's requirements and the basis of design, before it goes to construction.
  2. Systems verificationThe installation inspected and verified against the design intent, with a pre-functional checklist for each system.
  3. Functional testingEach system run through its sequences and measured, under normal, standby and failure conditions.
  4. Performance optimizationSystems adjusted after installation so that they run as efficiently as the design intended.
  5. DocumentationChecklists, test results, the issues log and the final report, in the owner's hands.

What commissioning covers

The systems and what is checked.

  • Air conditioning and ventilation

    Air handlers, condensing units, VRF systems, rooftop units, exhaust and the controls that run them: capacities, airflows, sequences and setpoints.

  • Electrical and emergency power

    Generators, transfer switches and uninterruptible power run through their transfer and return. The lighting controls through their scenes and schedules.

  • Plumbing and water heating

    Water heaters, recirculation and pumps checked for temperature, flow and controls.

  • Infrared surveys

    Electrical connections and mechanical equipment surveyed with a thermal camera by a Level II Thermographer, to find the hot spot before it becomes the failure.

  • Controls

    Sequences of operation tested against the design, with trending where the system allows it.

Definitions

What commissioning is and what it is not.

Three terms the market blurs. Defined here so that nobody assumes we are an inspection firm.

  • Commissioning

    Verifies that a building's systems are installed and perform as designed. Design review, verification of the installation and functional testing. We provide it.

  • Milestone inspection

    A structural inspection of condominium and cooperative buildings under Florida Statutes section 553.899, performed by a licensed architect or engineer. It is a structural service and it is not commissioning.

  • Building safety inspection

    Broward County's program asks for a structural report and an electrical report on buildings 25 years and older, then every ten years. It is a county inspection program, separate from commissioning.

Questions

Questions owners ask about commissioning.

The full set is on the Questions page.

Does RCI provide commissioning?

Yes. Our commissioning covers design review, verification of the installed systems and functional testing, through performance optimization and documentation. Brian Coldwell, PE, is a Certified Commissioning Authority.

What is a Certified Commissioning Authority?

A credential held by a professional who leads commissioning: writes the plan, verifies the installation, runs the functional tests and reports the results. Brian Coldwell, PE, holds it, together with a Level II Thermographer certification for infrared surveys.

Is commissioning the same as an inspection?

No. An inspection checks a building against the code at one moment. Commissioning verifies that the systems perform as the design intended, through tests the design itself defines. A milestone inspection under Florida law is a structural service and is not commissioning.

When does commissioning start?

At design. The design review is the first phase, so the questions are asked before the systems are installed rather than after.
