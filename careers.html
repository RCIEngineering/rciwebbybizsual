Why RCI

What you would work on.

In the record's own terms.

  • The work

    Residences from 5,000 to 26,519 sq ft. Restaurants, luxury retail, offices and medical buildings. Apartment buildings from six units to a campus of 280. A university in Grenada.

  • The tools

    AutoCAD and Revit. Projects that call for building information modeling are modeled in Revit.

  • The method

    An onboarding questionnaire, a basis of design set with the architect, structured weekly updates and a permit set signed by a Florida professional engineer.

  • The people

    Two founding engineers who still sign the sets, project managers who run each job and designers in every discipline.

  • The place

    An office in Davie, Florida, in the middle of the South Florida market we have designed for since 2005.

Open roles

The roles we hire for.

Whether a position is open at this moment or not, we read every application. Say which role and which discipline you are most interested in.

  • Project Manager

    Plans, coordinates and delivers MEP design projects: coordination, client communication, budget, timeline and the quality of the set that leaves the office.

  • Project Coordinator

    The organizational link of a project: scheduling, team coordination, document control and communication with clients and consultants, beside the Project Manager.

  • HVAC Engineer or Designer

    System design, load calculations and layout for the mechanical discipline, coordinated with the rest of the set.

  • Electrical Engineer or Designer

    Power distribution, lighting design, emergency and standby power and controls, from the one-line to the panel schedules.

  • Plumbing Engineer or Designer

    Domestic water, sanitary and storm systems, fuel gas and the risers that carry them through the building.

  • Fire Protection Engineer or Designer

    Fire sprinkler and fire alarm design, hazard analysis and code compliance under the Florida Fire Prevention Code.
